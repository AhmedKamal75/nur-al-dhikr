# Using Nūr al-Dhikr

A practical guide: installing the app, finding your way around, and
getting the most out of each section. No account, no setup wizard —
everything below works offline after your first visit.

## Install it

Nūr al-Dhikr is a PWA (progressive web app): a website that installs
like an app and then runs without internet.

1. Open the app in your browser (see [README.md](README.md#quick-start)).
2. **Android (Chrome):** menu → _Install app_ / _Add to Home screen_.
3. **iPhone/iPad (Safari):** Share → _Add to Home Screen_.
4. **Desktop (Chrome/Edge):** the install icon in the address bar, or
   menu → _Install_.
5. Open it once while online so it can cache itself — after that,
   airplane mode is fine.

Your data (favorites, counters, bookmarks, settings) lives in the
browser you installed it in. It never leaves your device. **Keep a
backup**: Settings → backup → download the JSON file, and store it
somewhere safe. Restoring is the same screen in reverse, with a
health check before anything is applied.

## Home

Home is your dashboard: greeting, Hijri date, next prayer, quick-action
tiles, and content panels (continue reading, daily progress, verse and
hadith of the day, and more). New installs start calm — a handful of
panels — and you opt into the rest: **Settings → Home panels**, where
every panel can be shown, hidden, and reordered. Nothing on Home
shames you for streaks; the nudge card suggests, never scolds.

Getting-started steps appear here on first run (set location, pick a
theme, install the app, read your first dhikr) and disappear on their
own when done.

## Adhkar & Duas

- **Library** holds the nine collections: morning, evening,
  post-prayer, sleep, tasbihat, duas, prophetic duas, Qur'anic duas,
  the 99 Names, and more.
- Each card shows its source and authenticity grade. An _Unknown_ grade
  means exactly that — unverified, not weak.
- Tap the counter pill (or anywhere on the card, per your settings) to
  count; **Focus mode** gives you one card at a time, full screen.
- **Search** understands Arabic without diacritics. **Moods** files
  cards by how you feel (anxious, grateful, …).
- ⭐ favorites, 📁 collections, 📤 share-as-image cards, 🔊 listen-aloud.
- **Editor**: add your own adhkar, edit text, hide what you don't use —
  your edits layer over the bundled books and "restore defaults" always
  brings the originals back.

## Qur'an

Two readers, one tap apart:

- **Mushaf** (the default): the 604-page printed layout with a choice
  of paper editions and Naskh typefaces. Pinch to zoom, swipe or tap to
  turn pages, double-page spread on wide screens, true fullscreen for
  distraction-free reading, and a translation tray.
- **Reader**: the study view — 5 translations, immersive mode, per-word
  grammar (tap any word), root browser, tafsir tabs, tajweed coloring
  with drill mode, and memorization tools.

**Tajweed colors** follow the standard chart: gray = silent, green =
nasal (ghunnah, ikhfa, iqlab, idgham), light blue = qalqalah, dark
blue = heavy, **red = elongation** (deeper red = longer). Tapping any
word explains exactly which rule applies to it and how to pronounce it.
Per-word colors can be toggled in Settings.

**Audio**: 314 reciters. Stream a surah, or download it for offline
listening. Verse-by-verse mode highlights each ayah as it plays, with
repeat, compare-two-reciters, echo (listen-and-repeat), sleep timer,
and speed controls.

## Prayer, Qibla, Calendar

- **Prayer**: set your location once (or pick a saved profile). Times
  are computed astronomically for seven calculation methods, with
  high-latitude fallback where the sun misbehaves. Enable per-prayer
  adhan alerts — sound needs the tab open with one prior tap (browser
  rules, not ours). Log prayers to build streaks honestly.
- **Qibla**: live compass bearing with calibration help.
- **Calendar**: Hijri months with your own notes and reminders, the
  voluntary-fasting tracker (Monday/Thursday, White Days, Ashura,
  Arafah), and timetables you can print.
- **Ramadan**: suhoor/iftar countdown and alerts in season, fasting log,
  and a Ramadan khatma preset.
- **Zakat**: step-by-step calculator with saved assessments and hawl
  anniversary reminders.

## Practice & progress

- **Tasbih**: pick a phrase, tap the dial (haptics if your device
  allows), set targets. Bursts survive reloads and closed tabs.
- **Statistics**: streaks, heatmap, and charts — descriptive, never
  judgmental.
- **Khatma planner**: set a target date or daily pages; the Mushaf
  marks pages as you read them.
- **Quiz, hifz review, journal, certificate**: small tools for
  memorization and reflection, tucked where you'd expect them.

## Make it yours

- **Language**: full English/Arabic interface with proper right-to-left
  layout — switch anytime in Settings.
- **Appearance**: light, dark, or auto theme; ten color palettes ×
  eight shapes; high-contrast and reduced-motion modes; text scaling
  including Arabic-specific sizing.
- **Kids mode**: big tiles, short surahs, stars for finished listens,
  hold-to-exit so little hands stay put.
- **Family profiles**: each person keeps their own favorites, counters,
  and streaks on the shared device.

## FAQ

**Is my data safe?**
It never leaves your device — there is no server to leak it. Back up
the JSON file occasionally; clearing browser data erases everything.

**Does it work offline?**
Fully, after the first online visit. Recitation audio streams unless
downloaded; everything else is on-device.

**Why is the Hijri date off by a day?**
The app uses the tabular Islamic calendar; your locality follows moon
sighting. Both are legitimate — adjust mentally by a day when needed.

**Why no adhan sound when the tab is closed?**
Browsers only let pages play audio while open (and after one tap).
Closed-tab alerts arrive as silent system notifications where the
platform supports them — the Prayer screen's reliability row tells you
exactly which path is live on your device.

**Something looks wrong in a text?**
Report it with the source. Sacred texts are fixed from citable sources
only — never from memory — and an "Unknown" grade beats a confident
error every time.
